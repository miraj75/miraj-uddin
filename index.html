“My journey has taken me from Bangladesh to Romania and then to Italy. Along the way, I have experienced different education systems, cultures, languages and everyday life in Europe.”

Through this website, I document my education, travels, experiences and life while sharing useful information with other Bangladeshi students who are interested in studying in Italy and Europe. This is my digital life — a living journal that grows with every city, every semester, and every story.

What I share
University applications, visas, scholarships, accommodation, jobs, cost of living, and real student life — from personal experience.
